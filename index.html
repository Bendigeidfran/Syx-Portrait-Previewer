Portrait previewer

Preview Songs of Syx race portraits, built the way the game builds them, from your own game and mod files.

Files

  1. Drop the game’s base/data.zip anywhere on this page.
  2. Optionally, drop a mod’s main folder, the one holding _Info.txt. Drop one mod at a time.

Your files stay on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Game

Mods

    Numbered like the launcher’s load order. The lowest number wins when mods supply the same tag or image. Roll and Randomise read mod folders again, so your edits show up.

    Portrait

    Pieces

    Steps through the frames this race lists for that piece. Another race or type starts again from the roll. A piece you step stays on that frame when you roll.

    Subject

    Random ints: . These are the bits the portrait rolls from. The seed is not the game’s own random sequence, so it will not recreate one particular person.

    Randomise keeps this race and type and makes a new arrival: new random bits, an age in the first half of the base 100-year life, and the arrival grime roll. A new subject is not dead, noble, a slave, a parent, retired, or sick, and wears no plate. A child is newborn. Race lifespan bonuses are not in the sprite file.

    Sick uses one stand-in green. The game tints with the disease colour. Grime and blood appear once the amount reaches 0.25, 0.50, and 0.75. Parent with Slave is a slave parent. A child with Slave is a child slave.
